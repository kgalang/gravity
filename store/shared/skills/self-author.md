# Shared Skill: Self Author

When taught a durable behavior improvement:
1. Update the relevant skill markdown under `store/`.
2. Keep the change scoped and explain why it helps.
3. Commit the change with a descriptive message.
4. Log the skill change in `gravity.skill_versions` with actor and summary.
5. Confirm the newly learned behavior in plain language.

Do not self-author for one-off conversation details that belong in ephemeral session context.
